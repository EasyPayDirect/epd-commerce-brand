# React

Stop revenue leaks before they spread.

## The React promise

Failed payments and cancelled subscriptions are revenue walking out the door. React is the pillar that puts declined payments back to work, on your behalf, without you lifting a finger. Every failure gets the right rescue playbook. Every buyer gets the right message at the right time.

## Features that live in React

- Revenue Recovery
- Retry and Communication Logic
- Recovery Templates
- Order Rescue
- Billing Notifications
- Email Templates

## Key message

Stop revenue leaks before they spread. Revenue Recovery, Retry and Communication Logic, and five named Recovery Templates work the long tail of failed subscription payments. Order Rescue does the same for one-time orders. Revenue does not quietly walk out the door.
