# Create

Build the products, plans, and pages that sell for you.

## The Create promise

Every product, plan, and page you ship should be ready for the buyers you have today and the AI agents that will buy from you tomorrow. Create is the pillar for building your catalog and everything around it, and every surface here is built to ship fast and stay aligned with the rest of the platform.

## Features that live in Create

- Products
- Landing Pages
- Subscription Templates
- Coupons
- Shipping Options
- Custom Fields
- Agentic Commerce (Seller Profile, ChatGPT Storefront, Google Merchant Center)

## Key message

Every product is built for the buyers you have and the agents that will buy tomorrow. Schema markup, structured data, and AI Channels ship on every product page by default. Landing Pages are AI-indexed the moment they are published, ready for ChatGPT, Google Merchant Center, and every other shopping agent your customers use.
