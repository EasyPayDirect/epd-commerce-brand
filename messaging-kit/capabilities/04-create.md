# Create

## Products, subscriptions, and AI-optimized pages

Launch a catalog that people and AI shopping tools can understand.

- AI-optimized landing pages with schema markup, llms.txt, and structured data.
- Subscription templates and dunning automation.
- Import products from your website to launch your catalog quickly.

## Notes for writers

- Feature names in this capability: AI Product Landing Pages, Custom Fields. Exact spellings are in `data/names.json`.
- Structured data makes products easier for AI systems to understand. It does not guarantee placement or ranking in any AI assistant. Never imply it does.
- "Dunning automation" is approved here because it is a recognized term for this audience. In merchant-facing body copy, prefer "automated retries for failed payments."
