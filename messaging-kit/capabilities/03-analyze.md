# Analyze

## Chat with your data. Let The Professor take action.

A personalized agent connected to transaction, gateway, and integration data. Ask in plain English, get answers grounded in your numbers, and have The Professor do the work.

- Explore transactions, declines, churn, and recovery conversationally.
- Get answers that cite the underlying records.
- Create products and subscriptions, charge customers, and configure revenue recovery.

## Notes for writers

- The feature is **The Professor**. Never "The Data Professor." See `data/names.json`.
- Attribute actions to The Professor by name, not to "AI."
- Numbers shown in The Professor demos and screenshots are sample data. Never reuse them as claims.
