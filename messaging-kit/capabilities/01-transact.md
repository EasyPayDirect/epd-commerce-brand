# Transact

## Payments first: the right merchant account and the ways buyers want to pay

Processing is the foundation of EPD Commerce. Businesses can take card payments on their existing website and connected carts, with checkouts and payment links as extra places to sell.

- **Easy Pay Direct:** a fully underwritten merchant account with thorough underwriting, a dedicated point of contact, and a setup designed for long-term processing.
- **Process Now:** auto-boarding for low-risk, lower-volume businesses (generally under $25K a month) that want to start quickly. Approval is often same day.
- Buyers can pay with Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, Venmo, ACH, and buy now, pay later (Splitit, Klarna, Afterpay, Sezzle), on either rail.
- Branded payment links and checkouts, plus metered billing (beta) for usage-based pricing.
