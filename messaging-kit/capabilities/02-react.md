# React

## Protect the revenue you already earned

Keep processing when a processor goes down, and recover failed subscription payments automatically instead of just being told they failed.

- Route transactions across merchant accounts so another account can keep processing if one goes down.
- Configure retry schedules, including same-day retries for soft declines.
- Send branded emails for failed payments, expiring cards, and retry notifications.
- Review every failed payment, retry manually or reach out, and track revenue at risk.

## Notes for writers

- Feature names in this capability: Revenue Recovery, Recovery Templates, Order Rescue, Optimized AI Strategy, Retry & Communication Logic, Billing Notifications, Email Templates. Exact spellings are in `data/names.json`.
- Never promise that every failed payment is recovered. Recovery depends on why the payment failed.
- Transaction routing requires more than one connected merchant account. Do not imply it works with a single account.
