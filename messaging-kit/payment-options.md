# Payment options

EPD Commerce gives businesses two ways to process payments. Both run inside the same EPD Commerce account.

**Same platform. Same dashboard. Same catalog.**

## The two processing options

| | Process Now | Easy Pay Direct |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Auto-boarding for low-risk, lower-volume businesses that want to start quickly | A fully underwritten merchant account with a dedicated point of contact, set up for long-term processing |
| Best for | New businesses and low-risk verticals, generally under $25K a month | Established, higher-volume, regulated, or higher-risk businesses, and anyone who wants white-glove setup |
| Approval timing | Often same day | Typically 1 to 3 days, depending on how quickly documents arrive |
| Underwriting | Automated | Full underwriting with a dedicated risk and compliance team |
| Routing | Single account | Multi-processor routing with backup account failover |

Process Now is not suited to high-volume, high-risk, regulated, or restricted businesses. Those businesses belong on Easy Pay Direct.

## Timing and fit language

Keep the soft words that are part of each claim. Exact wording lives in `data/claims.json`.

- Process Now: approval is **often** same day.
- Easy Pay Direct: approval **typically** takes 1 to 3 days.
- Process Now fits businesses **generally** under $25K a month. It is a recommendation, not a cutoff. Easy Pay Direct does not require $25K a month.

## Processing options vs payment methods

These are different things. Never mix them up.

- A **processing option** (or **processing rail**, EPD Commerce only) is the account behind the payments: Process Now or Easy Pay Direct.
- A **payment method** is what the buyer pays with.

Buyers can pay with cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, Venmo, ACH, and buy now, pay later (Splitit, Klarna, Afterpay, Sezzle) on either option.

## Works with an existing website

Businesses do not need to rebuild their website to use EPD Commerce. They can take payments on their existing website and connected carts, and add EPD Commerce checkouts and payment links as extra places to sell.

## Moving between options

A business can start on Process Now and move to Easy Pay Direct as volume, risk profile, or routing needs grow. Products, customers, subscriptions, and reporting stay where they are. Approved line: "Change rails without changing how you operate."

## Partners: one link for both options

Partners share one EPD Commerce partner link. They do not need to decide which option a business needs. The EPD Commerce team reviews each application and talks with the business about which option fits.

Partner copy should say:

- "One link. We'll help each business find the right processing option."

Partner copy should never say:

- That a business is guaranteed Process Now or same-day approval.
- That the partner decides which option a business gets.

## Naming

- Always name **Process Now** when referencing the auto-boarding option. "Near-instant approval" can describe it but never replace the name.
- When naming or comparing the options, call the underwritten option **Easy Pay Direct**.
- "Merchant accounts" is fine as a contextual eyebrow or label on social graphics (see `assets/examples/story-easy-pay-direct.png`), as long as the graphic names Easy Pay Direct elsewhere.
- Never mention banking partners by name. Say "one of our banking partners" if needed.
