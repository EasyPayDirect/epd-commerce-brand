# Custom Fields

## What it is

Custom Fields let you attach any data you need to any product. Text, dropdowns, dates, numbers, whatever the sale requires. The field carries through the checkout, the order record, and the customer file.

## How it works

Configure Custom Fields on any product. They appear at checkout, the customer answers them, and the values stay attached to the order for reporting, fulfillment, and follow-up.

## How to talk about it

- Always capitalize: **Custom Fields**. Never "custom fields" mid-sentence, never "custom field" singular in body copy.
- Attribute the behavior to Custom Fields, not to "the platform." Example: "Custom Fields captured the shipping preference at checkout."
- Do not describe it as "custom product attributes" or "extra form fields." That undersells the feature.
