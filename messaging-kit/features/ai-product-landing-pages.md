# AI Product Landing Pages

## What it is

AI Product Landing Pages generates a complete landing page from a product description. Sections, layout, copy, and imagery arrive together. You edit what you want and publish.

## How it works

Give AI Product Landing Pages a product description. It generates a full landing page with sections in the right order, sample copy for each, and layout placeholders for imagery. Every element is editable. When it's ready, publish to your storefront.

## How to talk about it

- Always capitalize: **AI Product Landing Pages**. Never "AI landing pages" or "AI-generated landing pages" as shorthand.
- Describe the outcome: a full landing page from a product description. Not "an AI writer" or "a copy generator."
- Attribute the behavior to AI Product Landing Pages specifically. Example: "AI Product Landing Pages built a three-section layout with copy and imagery."
