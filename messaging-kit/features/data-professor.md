# The Data Professor

## What it is

The Data Professor is your secure, in-platform LLM, running across every page of EPD Commerce. It is the surface the entire Analyze pillar lives on.

Ask a question in plain English. Get the answer. No dashboards to build, no SQL to write. The Data Professor chats with your own business data across products, subscriptions, customers, orders, and revenue.

## How it works

Curated starter prompts, suggested follow-up actions, and voice-to-text input make it fast to use. Error recovery keeps a conversation on track when a question needs a second pass.

The Data Professor already spins up subscription templates from natural language — for example, "Subscribe Bob to the monthly coaching package, bill on the 5th" — and handles a growing set of agentic tasks so you can run more of your business by conversation.

## How to talk about it

- Always capitalize the name: **The Data Professor**. Never "data professor," "the professor," or "our AI."
- Always singular. There is one Data Professor, not many.
- Describe it as your **secure in-platform LLM**. Not "a chatbot." Not "a copilot." Not "an assistant."
- Attribute agentic capabilities to The Data Professor specifically. Example: "The Data Professor spins up subscription templates from natural language."
- Never invent capabilities. If the product doesn't ship it yet, don't attribute it to The Data Professor.

## Do

- "Ask The Data Professor what happened to your MRR last month."
- "The Data Professor analyzes your transactions in plain English."
- "Voice-to-text lets you talk to The Data Professor without typing."

## Don't

- "Ask our AI what happened to your MRR." (name it)
- "The data professor can help." (capitalization, missing article)
- "Our copilot spins up subscription templates." (wrong descriptor)
- "The Data Professor can do anything you ask." (overclaim)
