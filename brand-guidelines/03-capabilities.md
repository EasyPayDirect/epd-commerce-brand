# 03 Capabilities

EPD Commerce uses four capability names, always in this order: **Transact, React, Analyze, Create.** Payments come first.

The approved copy for each lives in `messaging-kit/capabilities/`. Do not rewrite it from scratch.

## Rules

- **Never use a capability name alone.** Each name must appear with its one-line promise and supporting copy. A bare "ANALYZE" eyebrow with no explanation is not allowed.
- **Order is fixed.** Transact, React, Analyze, Create. Never reorder or skip one in a list of all four.
- **Always capitalized.** Never renamed, translated, or turned into verbs for other products.
- **Not in blog header images.** Capability names do not appear in blog header graphics. They confuse readers without the supporting copy.
- **Not a pillar.** The word "pillar" is retired. Say "capability" in internal docs. In customer copy, usually no category word is needed.

## The four promises

| Capability | Promise |
|---|---|
| Transact | Payments first: the right merchant account and the ways buyers want to pay |
| React | Protect the revenue you already earned |
| Analyze | Chat with your data. Let The Professor take action. |
| Create | Products, subscriptions, and AI-optimized pages |
