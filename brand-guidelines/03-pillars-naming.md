# 03 The Four Pillars: Naming Rules

Every capability in EPD Commerce maps to one of four pillars. This section is about how to talk about the pillars, not what lives in each one. For features and what they do, see the messaging kit.

## The four names

- **Create**
- **Analyze**
- **React**
- **Transact**

Always capitalized. Always in this order. Always these four.

## Rules

- Use the pillar names exactly. Do not rename, translate, or abbreviate them.
- Do not invent new pillars. If a new capability doesn't fit one of the four, either the copy is wrong or the feature is wrong. Come back to the four before adding.
- Pillar names appear as small uppercase eyebrows in DM Mono above section headlines, always followed by a sentence-case headline that explains the promise.
- Every named feature maps to exactly one pillar. Do not double-list a feature under multiple pillars.

## Pillar accent colors

Each pillar has a small accent color used for pill labels, eyebrows, and thin accents only. Never as a large surface fill. See `04-color.md` for the swatches.
