# 06 Voice and Copy

Grounded, plainspoken, contrast-framed. No em dashes. Ever.

## How we sound

Confident, plainspoken, grounded. Short declarative sentences. Talk to "you" and "your business." The product does things on your behalf. Never hype, never hedging, never exclamation points.

## Contrast framing

The brand often positions against the status quo. Two examples in current rotation:

- Most AI helps you write faster. EPD Commerce helps you run better.
- Most platforms tell you a payment failed. EPD Commerce does something about it.

## Do and don't

**Do**
- Use sentence case in headlines.
- Open features with a second-person imperative sentence fragment: "Your revenue command center." "Your full catalog."
- Title product features as proper nouns with periods: "Revenue Recovery." "Retry and Communication Logic." "Order Rescue."
- Ground claims in specifics. If a number can be verified, use it.
- Use commas, periods, or parentheses for pauses.
- Second person: you, your business.
- Say what AI actually does: analyzes transactions, recovers failed payments, helps you build products faster.

**Don't**
- Use Title Case Headlines That Feel Corporate.
- Open with abstract nouns: "A tool to manage revenue." "Solutions for your catalog."
- Lowercase feature names in body copy, or hedge them with generic words like "our recovery system."
- Use hype words: revolutionary, game-changing, supercharge.
- Use em dashes. They are off brand.
- Third-person marketing voice: users, customers.
- Say "AI runs the mechanics of your business." AI does not run every pillar. Do not overclaim.

## Naming conventions

- Product feature names are proper nouns. Capitalized wherever they appear. See `07-how-to-talk-about-features.md`.
- "agentic commerce" is lowercase in running text. Capitalize it only if it starts a sentence.
- Pillar names (Create, Analyze, React, Transact) are always capitalized. See `03-pillars-naming.md`.
- "EPD Commerce" is the product name. "Easy Pay Direct" is the parent brand. Never "EPD" as shorthand for Easy Pay Direct.
