# 02 Logo system

## The primary lockup

The horizontal lockup is the default. Diamond mark with white "EPD" inside, italic "COMMERCE" wordmark to the right. Use this everywhere unless space forces the stacked version.

## Writing the name

- Always write EPD Commerce. Never "EPD commerce", never "Epd Commerce", never "EPDC" in customer-facing copy.
- In body copy, spell it out on first mention. In tight UI or metadata, "EPDC" is acceptable.
- Never split the two words across a line break in headlines.
- Never use the diamond mark in place of the word "EPD" inline in body copy.

## Clearspace and minimum size

- **Clearspace**: keep an area equal to the height of the diamond mark on all four sides of the lockup. Nothing (headlines, logos, imagery, edges) enters that zone.
- **Minimum size for the horizontal lockup**: 120px wide on screen, 1 inch wide in print. Below this, use the mark alone.
- **Minimum size for the mark alone**: 24px on screen, 0.25 inch in print.
- Never letterspace the wordmark.
- Never stretch, squash, or rotate the diamond.

## What not to do

Common misuses. Any of these voids the brand. If a partner or vendor produces work with any of these problems, ask them to redo it.

- **Do not recolor.** The diamond is navy on light or white on dark. Never other colors, never gradients.
- **Do not stretch or distort.** Width and height scale together.
- **Do not add effects.** No glow, no shadow, no bevel, no stroke, no outline. The logo is a solid shape, even where cards around it use shadows.
- **Do not rotate or tilt.** The diamond sits upright as a rotated square. Never rotate further.
- **Do not place on busy imagery.** If a photo or pattern makes the logo unreadable, place the logo on a solid navy or white bar over the imagery.
- **Do not swap the wordmark font.** COMMERCE is a specific italic slab treatment. Never replace with a generic italic or bold sans.
- **Do not separate the mark from the wordmark.** In the horizontal lockup, the diamond and COMMERCE always travel together at the same spacing.
- **Do not use low-resolution raster.** For anything above 200px wide, use the SVG.
